# ![YouTube Local Feed](./src/icon48.png) YouTube Local Feed

**A private, local YouTube history and feed that works without relying on your Google account.**

YouTube Local Feed stores your viewing data inside your browser and gives you a simple YouTube-style feed with local history, subscriptions, playlists, analytics, and settings.

---

## Main Features

YouTube Local Feed includes its own YouTube-style interface.

- Home Tab
- Subscriptions Tab
- Shorts Tab
- Playlists Tab
- History Tab
- Channels Tab
- Analytics Tab
- Settings Tab

---

## Local History

Your watch history and progress are saved locally in your browser.

You can:

- See videos you have watched
- View saved progress
- Continue videos from where you stopped
- Keep history across different YouTube accounts
- Use the extension while logged out of YouTube

---

## Local Subscriptions

Subscribe to channels without using your Google account subscription list.

You can:

- Subscribe to channels locally
- Search for channels from inside the extension
- Subscribe directly from local search results
- Open channel pages and browse their videos
- View your subscribed channels
- Browse recent videos

---

## Playlists and Watch Later

Create and manage local playlists.

You can:

- Save videos to Watch Later
- Create playlists
- Add or remove videos
- Open saved videos from the extension

---

## Import Your YouTube Data

YouTube Local Feed supports importing data from Google Takeout.

You can import:

- YouTube watch history
- YouTube subscriptions from `subscriptions.csv`

---

## Backup and Restore

You can export your data to a JSON backup file.

Backups can include:

- Watch history
- Progress
- Subscriptions
- Playlists
- Watch Later
- Settings
- Analytics
- Preferences

You can restore the backup later or move it to another browser profile or device.

---

## Analytics

The Analytics page helps you understand your viewing habits.

It can show:

- Total watch time
- Activity by day
- Activity by hour
- Completion rates
- Most watched channels
- Unfinished videos

Analytics data stays local in your browser.

---

## Privacy

YouTube Local Feed stores your data locally.

Local data can include:

- Watch history
- Video progress
- Subscriptions
- Playlists
- Settings
- Analytics
- Feed caches

YouTube can still receive normal requests when you visit or watch videos on YouTube.

---

## Settings

You can change settings such as:

- Light, dark, or system theme
- Accent color
- Default feed page
- History cleanup period
- Feed refresh interval
- Backup and restore
- Data import

---

## Browser Support

YouTube Local Feed supports:

- Google Chrome
- Mozilla Firefox
